Website Privacy

Effective date: October 21, 2026
Last updated: October 21, 2026

This covers this website, dogancanyucel.com, run by Dogancan Yucel, Poland, who is the controller of what is described here. The apps shown on it have their own policies: Turquoise AI: Calorie & Fitness and Cairn: Skill Tree.

1. The short version

  • No cookies. The site sets none, so there is no consent banner to click away.
  • Nothing is stored in your browser. No local storage, no session storage, no database on your device.
  • No third party sees you here. Opening a page makes requests to this site and to nothing else — no fonts from Google, no analytics, no embedded video, no social widgets, no advertising.
  • Nothing identifies you. What is counted is described below, and none of it can be traced back to a person or joined up across visits.

2. What is counted

Two tallies are kept, and both are totals rather than records of visits.

  • Where pages are read from. One row per day, country and city, holding a number: for example 2026-10-21 · PL · Wroclaw · 14. No address, no browser, no page, no time of day, and nothing that joins two requests together.
  • How much the apps' endpoints were used. One row per day, endpoint and outcome, holding a number. This is the apps calling this site; nothing about who called is recorded.

A single row of either tally describes a crowd, not a person, which is why it is not personal data and nothing here asks for your consent. Requests to the apps' endpoints are not counted as page views, and page views are not counted as endpoint use.

3. Who else is involved

  • Cloudflare hosts this site and serves every page. Like any host it sees the request as it passes — including your IP address — and keeps its own short-lived operational logs for security and to keep the service running. Cloudflare acts for us under its data processing terms. We do not copy those logs or keep your address ourselves.
  • Nobody else. There is no analytics service, no advertising network, no tag manager, no content delivery network for fonts or scripts. The typeface the site uses, Inter, is served from this site itself for exactly this reason.

4. If you write to me

The addresses on this site are ordinary mailto: links: your own mail program sends the message, and the site is not involved. If you do write, I keep your message and address for as long as it takes to answer you and to keep a record of what was agreed (legal basis: my legitimate interest in answering people who write to me). The contact form this site used to have was removed, and the endpoint behind it was switched off.

5. Links that leave

Links to the App Store, GitHub, YouTube, X and LinkedIn are plain links. Nothing is requested from those sites until you click, and once you do, their own terms and policies apply, not this one.

6. Your rights

Under the GDPR you may ask what personal data is held about you, have it corrected or erased, object to its processing, or complain to a supervisory authority — in Poland, the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (UODO). Because the tallies hold nothing that identifies anyone, there is normally nothing here to give you, correct or erase; if you have written to me, that correspondence is what a request would be about.

7. Changes

If this site ever starts using a cookie, loading something from another company, or keeping anything that points to a person, this page changes before that goes live, and the date at the top changes with it.
