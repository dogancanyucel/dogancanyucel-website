PRIVACY POLICY

Effective Date: August 15, 2026
Last updated: September 19, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Turquoise AI Calorie & Fitness. Dogancan Yucel is committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring that your personal and health-related data is handled securely and transparently. This Privacy Policy explains how Dogancan Yucel collects, uses, and safeguards your information when you use the mobile application (the "App") on iOS and Android platforms, including the Apple Watch and Wear OS watch apps (collectively, the "Service").

Please note that the website dogancanyucel.com is used solely as an informational landing page and host for these legal documents. The website itself does not collect, track, or store any of your personal data.

By using the Service, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

2. Information Collected Within the App

2.1 Information You Provide

When you use the Service, you may provide certain personal data, including:

  • Account Information: Name, email address, and subscription details (which plan is active and when it renews).
  • Text Inputs: Search history for food logging and interactions with the AI Chat feature.
  • Physical Characteristics: Basic demographic and biometric data you manually enter (such as current weight, target weight, height, and date of birth) to help calculate your fitness metrics.

2.2 Google Health Connect Data (Android)

With your explicit consent, the App requests read-only access to the following data types from Google Health Connect. Each permission is optional: you can grant or deny any of them, and the App works with whichever you allow. The App never writes to Health Connect.

  • Steps: Your daily step count.
  • Active Calories Burned: Calories burned during activities.
  • Distance: Distance covered during your physical activities.
  • Exercise Sessions: Workouts recorded by your watch or other apps, with their type and duration.
  • Floors Climbed: Floors climbed during the day.
  • Speed: Your walking and running speed.
  • Heart Rate: Heart rate readings during the day and during workouts.
  • Resting Heart Rate: Your resting heart rate.
  • Heart Rate Variability: Heart rate variability (RMSSD).
  • Oxygen Saturation: Blood oxygen (SpO2) readings.
  • Respiratory Rate: Breaths per minute.
  • Skin Temperature: Skin temperature changes measured by your watch, where your device supports it.
  • VO2 Max: Your estimated cardio fitness (VO2 max).
  • Sleep: Your sleep sessions and their duration.

How this data is used and where it is kept:

  • Your daily step count and active calories are saved to your activity history and synced to your account, so they appear in your activity summary and on your other devices. Your recent daily step counts and active calories are also included when you ask the AI coach a question or create your AI Weekly Report.
  • Last night’s sleep duration is written as a short note in your AI coach chat. Your chat is saved to your account, and the AI coach reads your most recent messages, including that note, when it answers.
  • All other data types listed above are read only to display them in the Health Connect card and its measurements page on your phone. They are not saved by the App, not uploaded to our servers, and not sent to any AI service.

Wear OS watch app. The Turquoise Wear OS app does not use Health Connect. It reads your daily step count from the watch’s own health service and, during a run you start, your location to draw the route and measure the distance. Your step count and the finished run’s duration, distance and calories are sent to your phone; the route is not saved. It writes nothing to Health Connect.

2.3 Apple Health (iOS and Apple Watch)

With your permission, the App reads the following data from Apple Health. Apple asks for each type separately: you can allow or deny any of them, and the App works with whichever you allow.

  • Steps and Active Energy (active calories only, never resting energy).
  • Walking + Running Distance, Flights Climbed and Walking Speed.
  • Heart Rate, Resting Heart Rate, Walking Heart Rate Average, Heart Rate Variability and Heart Rate Recovery (one minute).
  • Cardio Fitness (VO2 max), Blood Oxygen and Respiratory Rate.
  • Sleep, including sleep stages where your watch records them, and Sleeping Wrist Temperature.
  • Exercise Minutes, Stand Time and your Activity rings with their goals.
  • Workouts and their routes.
  • Mindful Minutes, Time in Daylight and Environmental Sound Levels.

Some of these types exist only on newer versions of iOS; on older versions the App does not ask for them.

What the Apple Watch app writes. When you start a workout on the Turquoise Apple Watch app, the workout is saved to Apple Health — and, for outdoor activities, the route recorded by the watch. It reads your heart rate, active energy, steps and distance (walking and running, cycling, swimming) during the workout. It writes nothing else to Apple Health, and the iPhone app writes nothing to Apple Health at all.

How this data is used and where it is kept:

  • Today’s step count and active calories are saved to your activity history and synced to your account, so they appear in your activity summary and on your other devices. Your recent daily step counts are also included when you ask the AI coach a question or create your AI Weekly Report.
  • All other types are read to show them on the Apple Watch card and its measurements page on your iPhone. They are not saved by the App and not uploaded to our servers.
  • AI coach and weekly report: when you ask the AI coach a question or create your AI Weekly Report, a short written summary of the week is included in that request: average activity rings, heart rate range and resting heart rate, sleep duration and stages, heart rate variability, cardio fitness, heart rate recovery, respiratory rate, blood oxygen, wrist temperature change and recent watch workouts. It is sent to the AI provider that answers the request, as described in section 5. The answer you receive is saved on your device.
  • Apple Health data is never used for advertising and never sold.

3. How Your Information Is Used

Dogancan Yucel uses the collected information exclusively to provide, maintain, and improve the core functionalities of the Service. Specifically:

  • Health and Fitness Metrics: Health Connect and Apple Health data are used to show your measurements, to count your steps and active calories in your activity history, to give live feedback during workouts on your watch, and, as a short summary, to let the AI coach and your AI Weekly Report take your activity (and, on iOS, your recovery) into account.
  • AI Analytics: To power the artificial intelligence engine which offers fitness insights, calculates nutritional needs, and tracks progress.
  • Service Provisioning: To manage your account, process subscription payments, and communicate with you regarding your service.

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

4.1 Strict Limited Use Policy for Health Data

Dogancan Yucel absolutely does NOT sell, rent, lease, or trade your personal health data to any advertising networks, data brokers, or any other third parties for marketing or advertising purposes.

The use of information received from Google Health Connect and Apple HealthKit will adhere strictly to their respective limited use policies. Your health data is accessed and utilized solely to provide the core fitness, health, and AI analysis features of the application.

The only exception is the AI provider that answers your own AI coach or weekly report request, as described in sections 2.2, 2.3 and 5; it receives a short summary of daily totals and measurements, not your raw health records.

4.2 Third-Party Service Providers

Dogancan Yucel may share non-health information with trusted third-party service providers (such as payment processors and cloud hosting providers) only to the extent necessary to operate the Service. These third parties are bound by strict confidentiality agreements and are prohibited from using your data for any other purposes.

Crash reports. When the App crashes, or when a step such as phone verification fails, a report is sent to Firebase Crashlytics (Google) so the fault can be found and fixed. A report holds the technical details of the fault, the App version, the device model and operating system version, and an identifier for this installation of the App. It does not hold your name, email address, meals, health data or anything you wrote.

Background photographs. If you choose one of the App's stock backgrounds (Forest, Ocean or Caribbean), the picture is loaded from Unsplash, which sees the network address the request comes from. A photograph of your own stays on your device and, if you use Apple Watch, is copied to your watch.

5. Ephemeral Processing of Media (Photos and Audio)

The App’s own AI features are answered by Google’s Gemini API through our server. Our server passes each request on and does not store it. If you add your own API key or your own server address, requests go directly from your device to that provider or server, under its own terms.

The App utilizes advanced AI (via the Gemini API) to analyze your meals and posture, as well as to transcribe voice commands.

  • Photos: Body photos uploaded for posture analysis and meal photos uploaded for calorie calculation are processed ephemerally (in-memory) during transit via the Gemini API.
  • Audio: Voice recordings captured via the voice button for food logging are transcribed into text instantly.

CRITICAL PRIVACY GUARANTEE: All such image and audio files are permanently and immediately deleted the moment the AI analysis or transcription is complete. These media files are never stored, logged, or saved on Firebase, any server, or third-party cloud storage, nor are they used for training AI models.

6. Location

The App uses your location for two features only: recording a run you start, and showing the weather where you are.

Live Run Mode

Your location is collected only while a run you started is in progress — including when the screen is locked or you switch to another app, with your device showing that location is in use — and never when no run is in progress.

  • What it is used for: the route is drawn on your own map inside the App, and the distance of the run is the length of that line. Pace follows from the same measurement.
  • Where it goes: the run saved to your account holds its duration, distance and calories (on iOS, also its steps), so that it appears in your activity history on your other devices. The route is drawn on the map while you run and is not saved to your account; on iOS, a run recorded on Apple Watch keeps its route in Apple Health. It is not shared with anyone, not sold, and not used for advertising.
  • Turning it off: the App asks for location permission the first time you start a run and works without it — you simply get no map and no distance. Permission can be withdrawn at any time in your device settings.

Weather

To show the local weather, your location is sent to the Open-Meteo weather service, which returns the current conditions. The App does not store your location for this. On Android the weather is also refreshed in the background for the home-screen widget and for a weather reminder you set. If you do not allow location, the App simply shows no weather.

Barcode scanning

When you scan a product's barcode, the number on the barcode is sent to Open Food Facts, a public, community-run food database, which returns the product's name and nutrition label. Nothing else is sent — no account, no name and no other data about you — though, as with any request over the internet, Open Food Facts sees the network address it comes from. The App does not send a barcode unless you scan one.

7. Advertising

The App is free to download and shows advertising supplied by Google AdMob. Advertising is what pays for the free tier.

  • Advertising is not personalised. Every ad request the App makes is sent as a non-personalised request, which tells Google to choose the advertisement from the context rather than from you.
  • No tracking permission is requested. On iOS the App does not ask for App Tracking Transparency permission and does not link advertising data to your account or to data from other companies’ apps.
  • What Google receives: to fill an ad request, Google receives a device advertising identifier and technical information about the device. Google’s own handling of that data is described in its privacy policy.
  • Removing advertising: a Turquoise Premium subscription removes advertising, and a separate Ad-Free subscription removes advertising on its own for readers using their own AI provider key.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Your structured database data (profile, fitness logs, and AI chat history) is retained only for as long as your App account is active.

Your AI coach chat and your AI Weekly Reports are kept on your device and, on Android, also in your account.

Preventing Trial Abuse (Secure Non-PII Retention):

To enforce our Terms of Service and prevent the fraudulent reuse or abuse of the 7-day free trial, a secure, one-way cryptographic hash (SHA-256) of your registered email address and hardware identifier (Device ID) is securely stored in an isolated registry when you delete your account. Because SHA-256 is a one-way mathematical function, this hash is completely irreversible,meaning your actual email address and device identifiers cannot be reconstructed, read, or identified by Dogancan Yucel or any third party. This anonymous hash is kept strictly to perform automated trial eligibility checks upon subsequent registration attempts.

Your Rights and Control:

  • Revoking Access: You maintain full control over your health data. You can revoke access at any time: on Android in the Health Connect app’s app permissions, and on iPhone in Settings › Privacy & Security › Health › Turquoise.
  • Account Deletion (Data Deletion Request): You can request the permanent deletion of your account and all associated historical data at any time directly within the application with a single click. Upon initiating this request, all your personal information, historical fitness logs, and chat history will be irrevocably erased from the Firebase databases. You may also request deletion by contacting support@dogancanyucel.com.
