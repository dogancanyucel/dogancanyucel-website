What it does

Coach

AI chat coach

Answers about training and macros, and rewrites the programme when the week goes sideways.

Camera

Food photo analysis

Point the camera at a plate; calories and macros come back estimated.

Voice

Voice meal logging

Say what you ate. No forms, no dropdowns.

Gym

Gym tracker

Sets, reps, rest timers, and the calories burned per session.

Body

Body scan

Composition and posture feedback. The photos are never stored.

Sunday

Weekly reports

What changed, what didn't, and what the plan does about it.

Siri

Siri & Shortcuts

Add water, log a weigh-in, start a timer or hear how the day is going — from Siri, Shortcuts or the Action button. On iPhone.

Memory

A coach that remembers

Lasting facts — an injury, a food you skip — kept as notes on your phone. You see every line and delete any of them.

Wrist

Apple Watch & Wear OS

Sets, water and runs from the watch, in step with the phone.

Your key

Bring your own AI key

Plug in an API key from your own AI provider account and the app runs on it instead of the included allowance.

Plans

Free allowance, or premium

Everything works inside a free daily AI allowance; a premium tier is there for anyone who wants more.
