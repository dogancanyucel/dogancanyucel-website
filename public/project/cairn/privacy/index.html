Privacy Policy — Cairn: Skill Tree

Effective date: October 8, 2026
Last updated: October 11, 2026

1. Who we are, and what this covers

This policy covers Cairn: Skill Tree ("Cairn", "the App"), made by Dogancan Yucel, Poland ("we", "us"). Dogancan Yucel is the controller of the personal data described here.

Cairn is still in development on every platform — macOS, Windows, Linux, iOS and Android — and is not yet on any store. This policy describes the App as it is built today. It will be updated before any platform is released, and before any feature that sends new data anywhere is switched on.

The website dogancanyucel.com only hosts information about the App and this document.

2. The short version

  • What you learn stays on your device. Your plans, lessons, review cards, notes, books, focus history and calendar are stored only on the device you use. We do not receive them.
  • Your account holds only who you are. Your e-mail address, an account identifier, when the account was made, and which plan you are on.
  • The AI coach runs where you choose. On your own AI account or your own computer, the App sends what the coach needs directly from your device to that provider; we are not in between. On the Cairn Cloud + AI plan, it goes through our server to Google's Gemini; our server passes it on and does not keep what you asked or what came back.
  • No advertising, no analytics, no tracking. The App contains no advertising and no analytics or tracking tools, and it does not sell or share your data for advertising.

3. Your account

Cairn asks you to sign in or create an account the first time it opens. You can use Sign in with Apple, Google, or an e-mail address and password. Accounts are run by Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore, services of Google.

  • Firebase Authentication keeps your e-mail address, an account identifier, the way you sign in, and — for an e-mail account — your password, which Google stores in protected form; we never see it. Firebase also records the technical information any sign-in service needs to work and to protect accounts, such as the time of sign-in and your IP address.
  • Sign in with Apple asks Apple only for your e-mail address. You can choose to hide it; then Apple gives us a private relay address instead of your real one.
  • Google sign-in asks Google only for your e-mail address and basic identity (the openid and email permissions). It happens in your own web browser.
  • Your account record in Cloud Firestore holds your e-mail address, the time the account was made, and which plan you are on. Only you can read it; the plan is written only by our server.
  • E-mail checks: to stop throwaway accounts, the App checks the domain of an e-mail address you sign up with against a list kept inside the App. Nothing is sent anywhere for this check. An e-mail account must be verified through a link Firebase sends you before it can be used.
  • Your session is kept in your device's secure storage (the Keychain on Apple devices), so you stay signed in.

Why: to give you an account and to know what your account is entitled to (legal basis: performance of our contract with you).

4. What stays on your device

Everything you create in Cairn is stored on your device only:

  • your skills, plans, steps, lessons and review cards, and your review history;
  • your answers to exercises and quizzes, and the coach's feedback on them;
  • your notebooks and pages, including pictures and drawings you put in them;
  • the PDF books you add (the App keeps its own copy), with your highlights and notes;
  • your focus sessions, the distractions you mark and their reasons, and your calendar blocks, goals and growth notes;
  • the short facts the coach remembers about you, which you can see and delete in the App;
  • your profile (a display name you choose and your weekly study time) and your settings.

Focus and other apps (Mac): while a focus session is running, the App notes which other app you switch to and for how long, when you stay in it longer than 15 seconds, to show you how often you leave your work. You choose which apps count as study. These names stay on your device; a short summary of them can go to the AI coach (section 5).

Signing out leaves this data on the device; signing in again finds it. Anyone who signs in on the same device can see it.

Syncing your data between devices is not available yet. If it is added, this policy will say so before it is switched on.

5. The AI coach

Cairn's coach is an AI model. You choose where it runs:

  • Cairn's own AI (on the Cairn Cloud + AI plan) — see below.
  • Your own AI provider account — you enter an API key from a provider you have an account with. The providers the App offers are Google Gemini, OpenAI, Anthropic, xAI, Mistral, DeepSeek, Moonshot (Kimi), Meta Llama API, Groq, Cerebras, Together AI, OpenRouter, NVIDIA and Ollama.
  • A model on your own computer or network (Ollama on your own machine). Then nothing leaves your own machines.

When you ask the coach something, build a plan, have a lesson written, or ask it to review an answer, the App sends from your device directly to that provider: your request, and the parts of your Cairn data the coach needs to answer — for example the name of what you have open when you ask (a step, a review card, a book and its page number, a note page's title), your plans and steps, the lesson in question, cards you keep forgetting, recent exercise mistakes, your notes on steps, the facts it remembers about you, a summary of your focus sessions (including the names of the apps you spent time in), your calendar, your goals and the reasons you gave for skipping a block. If you ask about a passage in one of your books, that passage and its page are sent with that question only. Your profile name is not sent.

The provider processes this under its own terms and privacy policy, in your account with them. We do not receive, store or see these requests or the answers.

Your API key is stored only in your device's secure storage. It is sent only to the provider it belongs to, to make your requests, and never to us.

Cairn's own AI (Cairn Cloud + AI)

On the Cairn Cloud + AI plan the coach runs on Cairn's own AI, with no key to set up. The App then sends the same request and the same parts of your Cairn data, with your account's sign-in token, to our server (Google Cloud Functions, in Warsaw, Poland). The server checks your plan and your allowance, and passes the request to Google's Gemini model through Google Cloud Vertex AI, which answers it. Vertex AI is reached through its global endpoint, so Google may process the request outside Poland and the EEA.

  • What our server does not keep: what you asked, the data sent with it, and the answer. They pass through it and are not stored.
  • What it keeps for your account: how much of your allowances you have used — the number of calls, their cost and their token counts for the current week of questions and month of course writing — in Cloud Firestore. Each new week or month replaces the last.
  • What it keeps without your account: the day's total calls and tokens for the whole service, and one line in the server's log for each answer with its kind (question, coach or lesson), the model, the token counts and the cost — no account, no text. Google Cloud's request logs for the server also record the time, IP address and the App's network details of each call; Google keeps these logs for 30 days by default.
  • Proof that the request comes from Cairn (App Check): with each call to Cairn's AI your Apple device creates a DeviceCheck token, which Apple can check and which tells it the request comes from the real Cairn app on a real device. Firebase App Check (Google) exchanges it with Apple for a short-lived token the server checks. The token says nothing about you or what you asked.

Why: to give you Cairn's AI as part of the plan you chose, and to keep it within what the plan allows (legal basis: performance of our contract with you).

6. Other things the App does on its own

  • Checking lesson sources: a lesson can list websites, books and videos to look at. Before showing one, the App opens the address to check that it exists; for a YouTube video it asks YouTube for the video's title. These requests come from your device, so those websites see your IP address, as they would if you opened the link yourself. They carry no information about you.
  • Speaking and listening (language skills): reading words aloud uses your device's own voices. When you choose to answer out loud, your speech is recognised on your device by the operating system; the sound is not recorded, stored, or sent to us or to any AI provider. The first time, your device may download the language's recognition model from Apple. Only if you then press "Ask the coach" is the recognised text sent, as text, to your AI provider.
  • Permissions: the App asks for the microphone and speech recognition only when you use speaking practice, and works without them.

7. Who receives your data

  • Google (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore) — your account, as in section 3.
  • Google (Cloud Functions, Vertex AI, Firebase App Check) and Apple (DeviceCheck) — only on the Cairn Cloud + AI plan, when you use Cairn's own AI, as in section 5.
  • Apple or Google — only if you sign in with them.
  • The AI provider you choose — what you send to the coach, as in section 5, under your own account with them.

We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with anyone else, except when the law requires it.

Google and some AI providers may process data outside the European Economic Area. Where they do, they rely on safeguards the law recognises, such as the European Commission's standard contractual clauses or the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

8. How long data is kept

  • Your account and its record are kept until you delete your account.
  • On Cairn Cloud + AI, your account's use of the current week and month is kept until the next week or month replaces it, or until you delete your account. The server's log lines, which carry no account, are kept for 30 days.
  • Data on your device is kept until you delete it, delete your account, or remove the App. Skills, books, notebooks and pages you delete wait 30 days in Recently Deleted, then go for good; you can also empty it at once.

9. Deleting your data and your account

In the App, open Settings → Delete Account & Data. You can delete one kind of data at a time (skills, books, notes, focus history, calendar and goals, what the coach remembers, your AI keys), or tick Entire Account: this deletes your account and its record first — and with them what our server keeps of your use of Cairn's AI — and then everything Cairn keeps on that device. If you signed in with Apple, the App also asks Apple to remove Cairn from your Apple ID's sign-in permissions.

Data you sent to an AI provider is kept under that provider's own rules; you can manage it in your account with them.

10. Your rights

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation you have the right to access your personal data, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to its processing, and receive it in a portable form. To use any of these, write to support@dogancanyucel.com. You also have the right to complain to a data protection authority; in Poland this is the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (UODO), uodo.gov.pl.

11. Security

Everything the App sends over the internet is encrypted (HTTPS), except a connection you set up yourself to an AI model on your own private network. Your session and API keys are kept in your device's secure storage. Your account record can be read only by you.

12. Children

Cairn is not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly create accounts for them. If you believe a child under 16 has an account, write to us and we will delete it.

13. Changes to this policy

When this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above changes. A change that sends new data off your device is written here before that feature is switched on.
