Cairn:
Skill Tree

Learn any skill as a path of steps. A coach writes the lessons, spaced review keeps them in memory, and your own books sit beside them. Every platform is still in development; there is nothing to download yet.

Not on any store yet · The Mac app comes first

What it does

Plans

A skill as a path

Each skill is a board of steps in order. The next step lights up when the one before it is done.

Coach

An AI coach

Builds a plan from one sentence, writes each lesson, quizzes you and reviews your exercises. Ask it from any screen: it knows the step, card, book page or note you have open.

Review

Spaced review

Every lesson becomes cards, brought back just before you would forget them.

Library

Your own books

Add a PDF, read it, highlight and take notes, and ask the coach about the page you are on.

Notes

Notebooks and pages

Text, tables, pictures and drawings, in notebooks you organise yourself.

Focus

A focus timer that notices

Timed sessions, the moments you got distracted and why, and a week-by-week picture of your attention.

Your AI

Your own AI, or Cairn's

The coach runs on an API key from your own provider account or on a model on your own computer — or, on Cairn Cloud + AI, on Cairn's own AI with nothing to set up.

Badges

Badges that mean you remember

A skill's badge comes from your review record, not from clicking through it.

Plans

Free

The whole app

Every feature, with what you learn kept on your device, and the coach on your own AI key or a model on your own computer.

Cairn Cloud

On every device

Sync and backup across your devices, with the coach still on your own key. Sync is being built.

Cairn Cloud + AI

With Cairn's own AI

Everything in Cairn Cloud, and Cairn's AI with no key: a weekly allowance of questions to Ask the Coach and a monthly allowance for writing courses.
